How the  Sands of Time Slips Through: Vertical Slice

3 O'clock Studios

In the vertical slice you playthrough the level one puzzle scene, where the player has to play the proper tune on the piano to continue through the story. 

Player Controls: 
 - Move: WASD
 - Mouse: look around
 - Go BACK in time: left arrow key
 - Go FORWARD in time: right arrow key
 - Interact with Items: right mouse click
 - Throw Items: left click

Key Mechanics: player movement, change scene with hourglass, inventory system, on-screen display puzzle mechanic

Special Instruction: The cutscenes introducing the character context, and that cinematically reveal the puzzle answer have not been implemented in this version. This fraction of the story revolves around their first nostalgic reminiscence of their time living with their mother, as they hear her play on the family's piano.
